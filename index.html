Made for the sales floor

  • One-click ownership. Claim an available lead. A manager can reassign with a reason. Production claiming must prevent two simultaneous claims.
  • One buyer history. Keep every enquiry and source, link repeat enquiries, and review possible duplicates before merging contacts.
  • A short pipeline. New → Contacting → Qualified → Deal in progress → Won / Lost. Test drives, finance and trade-ins are separate fields, so cash buyers can skip finance.
  • A next action on active deals. Calls, appointments and follow-ups have an owner and a due time. Unclaimed and overdue work stays visible.
  • Notes stay internal. A comment is separate from a contact attempt or an outbound message. The live system records who changed what and when.

Useful answers for the owner

  • Is anyone waiting? See unclaimed leads, leads awaiting a first contact attempt, and overdue follow-ups.
  • What is likely to close? See deal stages, salesperson estimates, appointments, finance progress and expected vehicle sales value.
  • Who needs support? Compare team workload and follow-up completion in context, with clear ownership on each deal.
  • Which sources lead to sales? Compare unique opportunities and won deals by original source. Add ad spend only after a verified spend feed exists.
  • Why did a deal fall through? Record a lost reason and retain the history. Separate a confirmed sale from a reservation or finance approval.

Build it in useful stages

Start with the daily sales workflow. Expand connections after proving each route.

01 · Confirm

Agree the workflow and verify access

Confirm team roles, current website/form provider and hand-offs, branch versus group lead routing, advertising accounts, inventory source, response targets and what counts as a won deal. Route service/general contacts outside the sales pipeline. Agree claim versus automatic assignment rules, and validate the highest-volume channels.

02 · Launch

Deliver the core CRM and verified lead forms

Sign-in, shared inbox, claiming, customer history, notes, tasks, probability, pipeline, owner dashboard and basic connection-health monitoring. Pilot with website enquiries and whichever ad-form connections have passed access checks.

03 · Expand

Add messaging, stock context and better attribution

Connect eligible DM and WhatsApp routes, then approved inventory/marketplace feeds. Add campaign spend, booked test drives, finance hand-offs and reporting as their data sources are validated. Scope, cost and timing follow the access audit; no implementation quote is implied here.

The proposed foundation

Fits the Vercel + Supabase + GitHub workflow already used for your projects.

Vercel

The responsive CRM interface and authenticated server endpoints. Separate preview and production environments.

Supabase

Sign-in, PostgreSQL data, protected file storage and role-based access rules. Optional realtime updates for the team.

GitHub

Versioned source code, reviewed changes, database migrations and checks before deployment.

Proposed intake path: verified webhook → durable event queue → validation and duplicate checks → contact / enquiry / opportunity → assignment and follow-up. Retries, source event IDs, failure alerts and reconciliation protect against missing or duplicated leads. Credentials stay on the server.

Data handling is part of the build: least-privilege access, audit history, retention/deletion rules, consent and marketing preferences, and a tested backup/restore plan. Restrict finance documents to authorised staff. Review the final privacy and marketing process against POPIA before launch.

How the owner metrics are defined

Unclaimed

Open opportunities without an assigned salesperson. A person can have several messages without becoming several opportunities.

Overdue follow-ups

Open opportunities with an incomplete next action before the sample clock: 15 October 2026 at 10:00 SAST. Claimed active deals should always have a next action.

Weighted open value

Sum of each active deal's vehicle value multiplied by its estimated probability. Closed deals are excluded. It is neither revenue nor gross profit.

Closed-deal win rate

Won ÷ (won + lost). This demo uses all closed sample deals in October 2026. Production uses the selected closure period, rather than mixing new leads with old sales.

First contact

A logged human contact attempt after receipt. Internal notes and automated acknowledgements do not count. Production can measure business-hour response time once opening hours and qualifying events are agreed.

Source performance

Original source of each opportunity; later touchpoints remain in the history. The demo does not claim to measure ad spend, ROI or real dealership performance.
